What does 99.9% website uptime mean?

Understand uptime targets, downtime budgets and sampled monitoring.

Why it matters

An uptime target is a percentage of time a service is available. In a 30-day month, 99.9% allows 43.2 minutes of downtime; 99.99% allows 4.32 minutes.

How to set it up

Define the measurement window and what counts as unavailable. Track incident start and recovery alongside scheduled check results.

What to verify

BeepNado reports observed check success rates. Samples are useful evidence but cannot establish continuous availability or contractual SLA compliance on their own.
