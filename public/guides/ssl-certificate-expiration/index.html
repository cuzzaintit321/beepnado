How to prevent SSL certificate expiration

Monitor TLS certificate validity and renewal deadlines.

Why it matters

An expired certificate can prevent browsers and API clients from establishing trusted connections even when the web server itself is running.

How to set it up

Add an SSL certificate monitor for your public hostname and choose a renewal warning threshold. Check the certificate chain and hostname as well as the expiration date.

What to verify

After renewal, verify the certificate actually served by the public endpoint. A certificate installed on disk is not proof that the proxy has reloaded it.
