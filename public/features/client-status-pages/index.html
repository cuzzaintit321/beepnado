BEEPNADO FEATURE GUIDE

Client status pages and reports

Share the monitors each client needs to see and turn recent checks into a report.

A client page groups selected monitors into one read-only status view. Start with a draft, then publish it when you’re ready to share the link. The same monitor selection can produce a 7- or 30-day report.

How to use it

In Client pages, give the page a title, choose its monitors, and publish it to share a read-only link. Open the saved page to generate a report.

For a monthly update, use the 30-day report to share recorded check pass rates and incidents. You can copy the report or download it as text.
