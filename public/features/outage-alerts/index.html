BEEPNADO FEATURE GUIDE

Website outage alerts

Get outage and recovery emails even when the dashboard is closed.

BeepNado runs your checks on its server. When it confirms an outage, it records an incident and sends an alert to your verified email address. It sends another email when the check passes again.

How to use it

Verify your email address and add a monitor. You can close BeepNado once it’s set up. For an audible alert while you work, select Test sound, then Arm alarm and keep the dashboard open.

You can follow the incident into Investigate to see the evidence BeepNado recorded for the monitor.
