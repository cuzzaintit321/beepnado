BEEPNADO FEATURE GUIDE

Website outage alerts

See confirmed outages in your workspace and choose how you want to hear about them.

Confirmed outages appear in the dashboard alert feed. You can arm the browser alarm for an audible alert while the dashboard is open. With email delivery configured, verified accounts can receive outage and recovery emails.

How to use it

Open the workspace and select Test sound to hear the alarm. Choose Arm alarm and keep the tab open with audio enabled.

You can follow the incident into Investigate to see the evidence BeepNado recorded for the monitor.
