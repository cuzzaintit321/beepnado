QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What happens when my site goes down?

Checks run on BeepNado’s server even when you’re signed out. We record confirmed outages and send outage and recovery alerts to your verified email address. The audible alarm works while the dashboard is open and armed.

Do you send SMS, Slack, Discord, or webhook alerts?

BeepNado currently offers email, the dashboard alert feed, and an optional audible browser alarm.

Can I monitor staging or password-protected sites?

You can monitor a publicly reachable staging endpoint you’re authorized to check. Use an unauthenticated public health endpoint for services behind sign-in. Private network addresses are blocked.

What counts as a monitor?

One configured check counts as one monitor. That could be a website, a DNS record, a job heartbeat, or a Page journey of two to five public pages.

Can I pause alerts during planned work?

Schedule a maintenance window for a website monitor. BeepNado keeps the check records and suppresses outage and recovery alerts during that window.

How long do you keep check data?

Raw checks are retained for 30 days. Incident history is recorded separately. A published status page exposes the selected monitor labels, states, and incident dates.
